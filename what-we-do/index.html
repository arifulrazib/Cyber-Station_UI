Design notes

What changed compared with the live 3×3 grid, and why. No content was removed.

  1. Grouped by the site’s 5 domainsBuild · Protect · Detect · Test · Govern. These match the “Service architecture” section above, so visitors see one structure across the page.
  2. List and details side by sideChoosing a practice swaps the details panel. The page no longer jumps, and switching between practices takes one click.
  3. Flagship opens firstManaged Detection & Response is selected on load and marked “Flagship”. Before, it only had an orange border with no explanation.
  4. Colour means domainEach domain has one colour, used on its rail marker, icon and panel. Before, border colours cycled by column.
  5. Numbered badges removedThe 01–09 badges suggested an order that doesn’t exist. The domain label and the count of controls do that job now.
  6. Keywords shown as chipsThe comma-separated lists are now tags. Same words, faster to scan, and they still help SEO.
  7. Steps shown as bulletsThe single MDR and GRC controls held 5 steps each in one block of text. They now show as checklists, so the flagship no longer looks thin.
  8. Standards made visibleThe subtitle promises mapping to standards. “Maps to” tags are taken from the practice’s own copy (CIS, ISO 27001, SOC 2, OWASP, MASVS…).
  9. Every practice has a next stepEach one gets “Add to estimate” (to the calculator, pre-filled from data-service-id), “Talk to an expert” (opens the existing demo popup) and “Explore practice”.
  10. Accessible tabsReal button elements with role="tab", the panel linked by aria-controls, and arrow, Home and End keys. Below 960px the section becomes an accordion that uses aria-expanded.
  11. Phone layoutEach practice is a 64px row, grouped by domain, with one open at a time. The live layout stacks about 2,000px of cards.
  12. Copy cleaned upLabels now use sentence case (“SOC-AS-A-SERVICE” becomes “SOC-as-a-Service”), and the plural reads correctly (“1 control”, not “1 controls”).
  13. Content gaps on the live site“Awareness training” says “Describe this feature.” (placeholder copy is live), and three controls just repeat their own name as the description. The mockup hides those until real copy exists.
  14. Cards mention services that aren’t listedGRC mentions ISO, PCI and vCISO, but the only control is SOC 2. Network mentions Firewall, IDS/IPS and Zero Trust. Data mentions DSPM and UEBA. Intel mentions DFIR. Add those controls or trim the keywords.
